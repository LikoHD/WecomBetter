# Privacy Policy — WecomBetter 企微搭子

Last updated: 2026-09-13

Public page: https://likohd.github.io/WecomBetter/privacy.html

WecomBetter is a Chrome extension for WeCom Docs (https://doc.weixin.qq.com/). We do not run our own servers and do not collect personal data for ourselves.

## What we store

Only your feature on/off preferences (search, viewer avatars, related docs, author/time). These are saved with Chrome sync in your own Chrome/Google account. We cannot read them.

## What we access on the page

On WeCom Docs pages, the extension reads on-page collaboration info (who is viewing, avatars, referenced docs, author and edit time) to show the UI locally in your browser.

## Requests to WeCom

Search and “new smart doc” call WeCom’s own APIs using your existing login session. That traffic goes to Tencent/WeCom, not to us.

## What we do not do

No accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no selling or sharing data with other parties (except WeCom APIs you already use).

# 隐私政策

WecomBetter 企微搭子是用于企微文档的 Chrome 插件。我们没有自己的服务器，也不会把个人数据收集到我们这边。

只保存功能开关；页面协作信息只在本地显示；搜索和新建智能文档只调用企微自己的接口。不做广告、不做统计、不出售数据。
